Our Story

Two Hearts, One Journey

We didn't meet by chance so much as by a shared playlist and late-night chats about K‑pop. What began as friends cheering for the same groups turned into weeks of messages and small discoveries, and in 2016 we finally met in person, laughing like old friends from the very first moment.

From that shy beginning we've grown together into the team we are today, dating through the years and building a life side by side. As we reach 2028, our friendship has become the love we celebrate, and we can't wait to share that joy with you, our dearest family and friends.
